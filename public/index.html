    THE JOB

    Leonia posts 25 on almost every street and nobody drives it. You are not a police officer — you have no badge, no authority to detain, and no immunity. What you have is a radar gun, a dashcam, and the patience of a person who has watched the same intersection get run for years.

    The only currency that matters is standing. Clean, evidenced stops earn it. Pulling over someone you have nothing on burns it, and a town that has decided you're a crank stops cooperating entirely. You cannot enforce the speed limit at 70 mph, and the game will notice if you try.
